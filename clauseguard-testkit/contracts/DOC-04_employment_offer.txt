EMPLOYMENT OFFER LETTER AND TERMS

1. Position. Vertex Systems Pvt. Ltd. (“Company”) is pleased to offer Priya Nair (“Employee”) the position of Machine Learning Engineer, reporting to the Head of Data Science, with a joining date of 1 July 2026.

2. Compensation. Employee's annual fixed compensation is INR 1,800,000, payable monthly in arrears and subject to statutory deductions, with an annual performance bonus of up to ten percent.

3. Benefits. Employee will be entitled to eighteen days of paid leave per year, group health insurance, and a learning allowance of INR 40,000 per year.

4. Full-Time Dedication. Employee shall devote her entire working time to the Company and shall not, during employment, hold any other employment, consultancy, freelance engagement or side business, paid or unpaid, without the Company's written consent.

5. Post-Employment Restriction. For twelve (12) months after leaving the Company, Employee shall not join, advise or work for any company in India that develops or sells competing data analytics products, directly or indirectly.

6. Inventions. Employee assigns to the Company all inventions, models, software and works she conceives or develops during employment, including those developed outside working hours and without Company resources.

7. Termination by Company. The Company may terminate Employee's employment at any time, with or without cause and without prior notice or payment in lieu of notice.

8. Training Bond. If Employee resigns or is dismissed for misconduct within eighteen (18) months of joining, she shall pay the Company INR 300,000 as liquidated damages towards training and recruitment costs.

9. Resignation Notice. Employee may resign by giving ninety (90) days' written notice, during which she shall continue to perform her duties and assist with handover.

10. Non-Solicitation. For twelve months after leaving, Employee shall not solicit any Company employee to leave their employment or induce any client of the Company to reduce its business with the Company.

11. Confidentiality. Employee shall not disclose or use any confidential information of the Company during or after employment, except as required to perform her duties or by law.

12. Probation. Employee shall serve a probation period of six months, after which employment will be confirmed in writing subject to satisfactory performance.

13. Governing Law. This offer and the terms of employment shall be governed by the laws of India, and disputes shall be subject to the courts at Bengaluru.
