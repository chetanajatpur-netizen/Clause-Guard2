FREELANCE SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT
 AGREEMENT
 
1. Parties. This Freelance Services Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made on 1 March 2026
between Northwind Retail Pvt. Ltd., a company incorporated in India (“Client”), and Arjun Mehta, an
independent software developer (“Contractor”).
2. Scope of Services. Contractor shall design, develop and deliver the Client's mobile ordering
application in accordance with the specification attached as Schedule A, in three milestones over a
period of six months.
3. Fees and Payment. Client shall pay Contractor a fixed fee of INR 600,000, payable in three equal
instalments within forty-five days of invoice following acceptance of each milestone.
4. Exclusive Engagement. During the Term, Contractor shall work exclusively for Client and shall
not provide software development or consulting services of any kind to any other person or entity
without Client's prior written approval.
5. Non-Competition. For a period of twenty-four (24) months after the termination of this Agreement,
Contractor shall not, anywhere in India, directly or indirectly engage in, be employed by, or provide
services to any business that competes with Client's retail technology products.
6. Ownership of Work Product. All work product, inventions, code and materials created by
Contractor during the Term, whether or not related to the Services and whether or not created using
Client's resources, shall be the sole and exclusive property of Client, and Contractor hereby
irrevocably assigns to Client all right, title and interest therein.
7. Termination. Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or for no reason,
by giving Contractor twenty-four (24) hours' written notice. Contractor may not terminate this
Agreement before completion of all milestones without Client's written consent.
8. Liability. Contractor shall be liable for, and shall indemnify Client against, all losses, damages,
costs and expenses of any kind arising out of the Services, and Contractor's liability under this
Agreement shall not be subject to any limit or cap.
9. Delay Damages. If Contractor fails to deliver any milestone by its due date, Contractor shall pay
Client liquidated damages equal to two percent (2%) of the total fee for each day of delay, which the
parties agree is a genuine pre-estimate of Client's loss.
10. Assignment. Contractor shall not assign, transfer, delegate or subcontract any of its rights or
obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of Client. Client may assign this
Agreement to any third party at its discretion.
11. Inspection of Records. Client and its representatives may, upon reasonable notice and at any
time during the Term and for two years thereafter, inspect and audit Contractor's books, records,
time logs and source code repositories relating to the Services.
12. Confidentiality. Contractor shall keep confidential all non-public information of Client disclosed
in connection with this Agreement and shall use it solely to perform the Services.
13. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India, and the courts of
Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising from it.
14. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and
supersedes all prior discussions, representations and understandings relating to its subject matter.
