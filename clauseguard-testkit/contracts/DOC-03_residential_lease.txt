RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

1. Parties and Premises. This Residential Lease Agreement is made on 1 June 2026 between Meera Iyer (“Landlord”) and Rohan Das (“Tenant”) for the apartment at Flat 4B, Lakeview Residency, Bengaluru (the “Premises”).

2. Term. The lease term is eleven (11) months commencing 1 June 2026 and ending 30 April 2027, unless renewed in writing by both parties.

3. Rent. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of INR 32,000 on or before the fifth day of each month by bank transfer to the account designated by Landlord.

4. Security Deposit. Tenant has paid a refundable security deposit of INR 96,000, which Landlord shall return within thirty days after the end of the term, less lawful deductions for unpaid rent or damage.

5. Late Payment Charge. If rent is not received by the tenth day of the month, Tenant shall pay Landlord a fixed charge of INR 1,500 plus INR 200 for each additional day of delay as liquidated damages for the inconvenience and administrative cost of late payment.

6. Early Vacating. If Tenant vacates the Premises before the end of the term for any reason, Tenant shall pay Landlord all rent for the remainder of the term as liquidated damages.

7. Subletting and Assignment. Tenant shall not sublet the Premises or any part of it, nor assign this lease to any other person, without the prior written consent of Landlord, which Landlord may withhold in its sole discretion.

8. Landlord's Right to End Lease. Landlord may terminate this lease at any time, without giving any reason, by giving Tenant fifteen (15) days' written notice, whereupon Tenant shall vacate the Premises.

9. Damage and Liability. Tenant shall be responsible for, and shall compensate Landlord in full for, any and all loss or damage to the Premises and its contents caused by Tenant, its guests or pets, without any limit on the amount of such liability.

10. Landlord Inspection. Landlord may enter the Premises on forty-eight hours' notice to inspect its condition, carry out repairs, or show it to prospective tenants, at reasonable hours.

11. Utilities. Tenant shall pay all electricity, water and internet charges for the Premises directly to the relevant providers during the term.

12. Pets and Noise. Tenant may keep one domestic pet with Landlord's consent and shall not cause noise or nuisance to neighbours between 10 pm and 7 am.

13. Governing Law. This lease shall be governed by the laws of India and any dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the courts at Bengaluru.
