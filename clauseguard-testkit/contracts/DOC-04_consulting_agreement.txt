PROFESSIONAL CONSULTING SERVICES AGREEMENT

1. Engagement and Scope. Company hereby engages Consultant, and Consultant agrees to provide professional management consulting and advisory services as described in mutually executed Statements of Work.

2. Fees and Expense Reimbursement. Company shall compensate Consultant at the agreed hourly billing rate, and shall reimburse reasonable travel expenses incurred in performing the consulting services.

3. Exclusivity Commitment. Consultant covenants and agrees that throughout the term of this Agreement, Consultant shall act exclusively for Company in the digital banking sector and shall not provide advisory services to any competing retail banking enterprise.

4. Assignment of Intellectual Property. Consultant hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to Company all right, title, and interest in and to all work product, analyses, documentation, and inventions created by Consultant in connection with the consulting services.

5. Change of Control. If Consultant undergoes a Change of Control, including any corporate merger, acquisition, sale of substantially all equity or assets, or change in ownership of voting securities, Company may terminate this Agreement immediately upon notice.

6. Anti-Assignment. Consultant shall not assign this Agreement or delegate any performance hereunder to any third party or subcontractor without the prior written consent of Company.

7. Independent Contractor Relationship. Consultant is an independent contractor and nothing herein creates any partnership, joint venture, employer-employee, or agency relationship between the parties.

8. Indemnification Obligations. Consultant shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Company from and against any third-party claims arising out of Consultant's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

9. Notices. All formal legal notices under this Agreement must be sent in writing by certified mail or reputable overnight courier to the addresses listed in the preamble.
