CONSULTING AGREEMENT (FALSE-POSITIVE STRESS TEST)

1. Parties. This Consulting Agreement is made on 20 June 2026 between Lumen Advisory LLP (“Lumen”) and Sundar Textiles Ltd. (the “Client”) for operations improvement consulting.

2. Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within thirty (30) days of receiving notice of the breach.

3. Non-Solicitation. Neither party shall solicit for employment the other party's personnel who worked on the project during the term and for six months afterwards.

4. Financial Statements. Each party shall have its own annual financial statements audited by independent auditors of its choice and shall bear its own audit costs.

5. Task Allocation. The Lumen project manager may assign tasks to individual consultants based on availability and skills, and shall inform the Client of significant changes in staffing.

6. Expense Limit. Travel and accommodation expenses shall not exceed INR 50,000 per month without the Client's prior written approval, and shall be reimbursed on submission of receipts.

7. Interest on Late Payments. Overdue payments shall bear simple interest at one percent (1%) per month from the due date until the date of actual payment.

8. Exclusive Remedy. Re-performance of the defective services at no extra charge shall be the Client's exclusive remedy for any non-conforming deliverable.

9. Competition Law. Each party shall comply with all applicable competition and anti-trust laws in connection with this Agreement and shall not engage in any anti-competitive conduct with respect to its subject matter.

10. Pre-existing Materials. Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing materials, tools and methodologies, and grants the other a limited licence to use them solely for the purposes of this Agreement.

11. Control of Work. The consultants shall control the means and methods of performing the Services and shall remain under the control and supervision of Lumen at all times.

12. Limitation of Liability. Lumen's total liability to the Client under this Agreement, whether in contract or tort, shall be limited to the fees paid or payable for the Services giving rise to the claim.

13. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India and the courts at Chennai shall have jurisdiction.
