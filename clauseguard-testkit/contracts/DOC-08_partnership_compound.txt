PLATFORM PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT (COMPOUND CLAUSES)

1. Parties. This Platform Partnership Agreement is made on 5 August 2026 between Zenith Media Inc. (“Zenith”) and Kora Studios LLP (“Kora”) for the production of original series.

2. Term and Exit. Zenith may terminate this Agreement at any time on seven days' notice without cause. Kora may not assign this Agreement or subcontract production without Zenith's written consent.

3. Platform Commitment. During the term Kora shall create content solely for Zenith's platform, and for twelve months after the term Kora shall not produce similar programming for any competing streaming service.

4. Content Ownership. All content produced under this Agreement, including characters, formats and derivative works, belongs to Zenith and is hereby assigned by Kora. Kora shall indemnify Zenith against all claims arising from the content without limitation as to amount.

5. Liability and Accounts. Zenith's liability to Kora shall not exceed the fees paid in the preceding six months. Zenith may audit Kora's production accounts once a year on fifteen days' notice.

6. Ownership Changes. Any merger, acquisition or transfer of control of Kora requires Zenith's prior consent, failing which Zenith may terminate immediately and treat the change as an unauthorised assignment.

7. Late Delivery. If Kora delivers an episode late, Kora shall pay Zenith USD 10,000 per day of delay as agreed liquidated damages.

8. Payment. Zenith shall pay Kora a licence fee of USD 40,000 per episode within thirty days of delivery and acceptance.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York, and the courts of New York County shall have jurisdiction.
