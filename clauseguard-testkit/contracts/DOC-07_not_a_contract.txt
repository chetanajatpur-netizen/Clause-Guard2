1. Overview. Classic Lemon Drizzle Cake. This simple loaf cake serves eight people and keeps for three days in an airtight tin.

2. Ingredients. You will need 200 g butter, 200 g caster sugar, three eggs, 225 g self-raising flour, the zest of two lemons and 100 ml lemon juice for the drizzle.

3. Method. Heat the oven to 180 C and line a loaf tin. Beat the butter and sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then fold in the flour and zest. Bake for forty-five minutes.

4. Serving Notes. Serve exclusively on the day it is drizzled for the best texture. If the top browns too quickly, terminate the heat by turning the oven off and leave the cake inside for ten minutes.
